Support

Something not working, or a question we haven't answered? Email us at info@alignhealth.app. A real person reads every email and replies as soon as we can.

We read and answer every email ourselves. Tell us what you'd like to see next, or what could be better: info@alignhealth.app

We don't know who you are.

There's no account, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. We hold nothing about your health either. We can't see your data, share it or lose it, because we never had it.

That also means we can't look you up, so if you write to us, just tell us what's going on.

Not for you?

If AlignHealth isn't what you hoped, you can ask Apple for your money back at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles every payment and refund, so they decide, not us. But we'll never stand in your way.
