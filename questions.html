Questions

Why doesn't it feel personal yet?

The predictions learn you first. From the start it compares you with your own normal, and every answer makes it more yours. The reasons take longer, because a reason only becomes "from your days" once your own days have proven it, again and again. For some people that's a few weeks, for others a couple of months. The more often you answer, and the more you wear your watch, especially at night, the sooner it learns you.

What does "movement" mean in AlignHealth?

Not just steps. Movement is anything that makes your body work. Walking, of course, but also the effort your steps don't show: lifting and carrying, cleaning the house, washing the car, gardening, cooking on your feet, playing with the kids, cycling, swimming, the gym.

AlignHealth compares your movement only with your own usual days, never with a target like 10,000 steps. A quiet day for one person is a busy day for another.

It also notices how hard your heart worked for the same movement. The same walk can take more out of you on some days than on others, and that matters for how you feel.

Which Apple Watch do I need?

Apple Watch Series 4 or newer, or any Apple Watch SE, with an iPhone XS or newer. It needs iOS 17.6 and watchOS 10.6 or later. A Series 3 or older won't work.

Does my watch make a difference?

Yes. Newer watches measure more, and more often, so AlignHealth has more to learn from each day and usually gets to know you faster. It works on every watch above, but on an older one it may take longer to feel personal. Whichever watch you have, wearing it at night helps the most.

Why does AlignHealth ask for my location?

For three things. The weather where you actually were, so it knows if you were out in the cold, the rain or the heat. The places you spend your days, like home, work or out and about, because a day somewhere new can feel different. And the route of a workout, if you record one.

AlignHealth sends your location nowhere except a rough position, about 2 km across, to Apple's weather to get the forecast, and the area of a map you choose behind a workout picture. We never see it, and we never see where you go.

You can say no. AlignHealth still works, just without the weather and places.

Why did it stop asking me every day?

At first, AlignHealth asks each question every day, because your answers are how it learns what your days feel like. Once a question has 20 answers, or you've skipped it five days in a row, it starts predicting instead of asking. Every now and then it asks again without showing its prediction first, so it keeps learning from you and doesn't just agree with itself.

On any day, you can tap "yes, this is how i feel" or "not how it feels?" on a prediction. Every answer still teaches it.

What does "between rested well and rested normally" mean?

Some mornings you're not quite one or the other. When AlignHealth sees you right in the middle, it says so, instead of pretending to be sure of one word. If you feel either of the two, tap "yes, this is how i feel", and it counts as right. If neither fits, tap "not how it feels?" and pick the word that does.

What does "right 4 of 7 on days like this" mean?

Under each prediction you'll see a line like this. It means that on the 7 days most like today, the prediction was right 4 times. It's there so you know how much to trust today's prediction. If it says "the first day like this", the app hasn't seen a day like today yet, so take that prediction more lightly.

What is hrv, and why does it jump around?

Hrv is the tiny change in time between one heartbeat and the next. Your watch measures it, mostly while you sleep. Higher usually means your body is more at rest, but it moves a lot from night to night, so one night high or low means very little on its own. That's why AlignHealth only compares it with your own usual, and never treats one reading as good or bad news.

Do I need to wear my watch at night?

No, but it helps the most. At night your watch records your sleep and your heart at rest, and that's some of the most useful information AlignHealth gets. Without it, the app still works from your days and your answers, it just takes longer to get to know you. If you don't wear it, you can type your bedtime and waking time yourself.

Why is energy only an estimate?

Your watch doesn't measure calories directly. Apple works them out from your movement, your heart and your body details. So the number is best used to compare one day with your own other days, not as an exact count.

What stays free after the free month?

Steps, workouts, workout clips, your hours outside and every measurement stay free, always. The daily questions, the predictions and their reasons, patterns, changes and the weather need the one payment. It's £19.99 in the UK, and Apple shows the price for your country. Nothing is ever deleted. If you pay later, everything you recorded and everything the app learned is still there, waiting.

Is AlignHealth medical advice?

No. AlignHealth helps you understand how your own days make you feel. It doesn't diagnose anything or tell you what to do about your health. If you're worried about your health, a doctor is the one to ask.

What if it gets it wrong?

Tap "not how it feels?" and pick the word that fits how you really feel. Every correction teaches it, so the same kind of mistake gets less likely. It will still get some days wrong, because some days surprise everyone, and it tells you how often it's been right on days like today.

Does my family share one app?

The payment, yes: one payment covers up to six people with Family Sharing. The app, no. Each person's AlignHealth learns only from them, on their own phone. Nothing passes between your phones, and nobody in the family can see anyone else's days.

Does it drain my battery?

No more than you'd notice. Most of the work, learning from your days, happens at night while your phone is charging. During the day it mostly reads what your watch and phone already record.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything runs on your phone, so it works offline. Only the weather needs a connection, and when you're back online it usually fills in the hours it missed.

Can I take my data out, or delete it?

Yes, both, any time, from Profile. "download your data" saves everything AlignHealth holds in one file. "delete your data" removes it all from the app. Before deleting, the app offers to download it first, and with that file, "restore your data" brings everything back, on the same phone or a new one.

Deleting never touches your Apple Health data. If AlignHealth still has access to Health, it reads your health history again next time you open it. To stop that too, switch off its access in the Health app.

What about holidays, night shifts or a new job?

AlignHealth compares you only with your own days, whatever they look like. It learns from every day, all the time, whatever you do and whatever your work is. A week off, a run of night shifts or a new job are just more days for it to learn from, and it keeps every one of them.

Is it only in English?

Yes, for now.
