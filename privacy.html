Privacy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

AlignHealth has no servers and collects nothing. We have no way to see, keep or share your health data. It's yours, and you can export it or delete it at any time.

Who we are

AlignHealth, United Kingdom. Questions about privacy: info@alignhealth.app.

What the app reads

With your permission, the app reads Apple Health data such as steps, heart rate, hrv, sleep and workouts, plus your location and motion. It uses them on your phone to learn how your days make you feel. None of it is sent to us.

What the app writes to Apple Health

Workouts you record with your watch, their routes, and, for each heart scan you take on the watch, the heart and energy readings it measured. A scan is never saved as a workout. Nothing already in Apple Health is changed or removed.

What the app sends, and where

Weather comes from Apple Weather. To get it, the app sends a rough position, about 2 km, and the hours it needs. Nothing else, and never your health data. In a future update we may move to Open-Meteo, another weather service, for more detail. It would get the same rough position and hours, nothing more, and we'll say so in that update's notes.

If you choose a map behind a workout picture, Apple Maps is asked for that area, nothing more.

Your payment goes through Apple. We never see your card or your name.

Your choices

You can download or delete everything the app holds from Profile, and switch off its access to Apple Health or your location at any time in your iPhone's settings.

If this ever changes

We'll update this page and say so in the app's update notes first.
