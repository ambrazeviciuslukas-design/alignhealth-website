Questions

Is AlignHealth a fitness tracker?

No. Your Apple Watch already tracks your body. AlignHealth explains what those signals mean — inside your context, against your own history.

Does it compare me to other people?

Never. AlignHealth is built entirely around your personal baseline — your HRV range, your movement rhythm, your recovery patterns.

Is my health data private?

Completely. AlignHealth reads HealthKit data on your iPhone and processes it locally. No account. No servers. No sharing.

How does it get smarter over time?

AlignHealth builds a personal baseline from your data. The longer it runs, the more accurately it understands what normal looks like for you specifically — not the average person.

Do I need to wear my watch at night?

No. Sleep data helps, but AlignHealth works with whatever your watch collects. When evidence is limited, it tells you — rather than pretending certainty it doesn't have.

Which Apple Watch do I need?

Series 4 or later. AlignHealth uses HRV, heart rate, steps, and activity data — all available from Series 4 onwards.
