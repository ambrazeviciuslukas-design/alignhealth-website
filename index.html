How it works

  1. Wear your watch.Day and night if you can. It records your sleep, your steps and your heart, like it already does.
  2. Answer a few quick questions.How rested you are in the morning, how your day's going at midday, how tired you are in the evening. A tap each, or skip them.
  3. See your prediction and why.Every day it predicts how you'll feel and shows the reasons from your own days. The more it knows you, the better it gets.
