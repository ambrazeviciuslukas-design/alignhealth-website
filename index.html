AlignHealth

How you'll feel today, and why.

Every morning it predicts how rested you are. At midday, how your day is going. In the evening, how tired you are.

Then it shows you the reasons. Not a score with no explanation, but what made the difference, from your own days: your sleep, your steps, your heart, the weather you were out in.

No two people get the same app. It starts with what's true for most people, then it learns you, only from you. The more you wear your watch, the better it gets.

We keep nothing. No account, no email, no servers. Your health data isn't ours to see, so there's nothing of yours to lose.

Started by a courier in Liverpool. The ideas came from real days: long rounds in the wind, the rain and the heat.

Made for any kind of day, at a desk or on your feet.

How it works

  1. Wear your watch.Day and night if you can. It records your sleep, your steps and your heart, like it already does.
  2. Answer a few quick questions.How rested you are in the morning, how your day's going at midday, how tired you are in the evening. A tap each, or skip them.
  3. See your prediction and why.Every day it predicts how you'll feel and shows the reasons from your own days. The more it knows you, the better it gets.
